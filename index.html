Help / FAQ

Why do I see the OTP on screen?

This repository runs entirely in the browser. Showing the OTP keeps the demo testable without paid providers.

What happens after refresh?

Theme and remembered identifier are restored. Pending OTP sessions are restored only until they expire.

How do I use real OTP in production?

Move OTP generation and verification to a backend and use a verified email/SMS provider.
